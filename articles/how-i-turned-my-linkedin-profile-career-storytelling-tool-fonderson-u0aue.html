Solving the “How Do I Explain What I Do?” Problem with AI

As a doctor and researcher working across languages, systems, and disciplines, I’ve often struggled to answer one simple question:

“So what do you actually do?”

Not because I haven’t done meaningful work, but because most of it doesn’t fit into neat titles or short bios.

Like many medical professionals, my LinkedIn profile is long. Years of clinical experience, academic research, digital health tools, international transitions. It’s all there but when I need to tailor that experience for a grant, a new role, or a speaking opportunity, I find myself stuck.

Too much context. Not enough clarity.

As part of my journey to better understand LLM's I spent the next 3 weeks building to navigate through the course by Ed Donner on #LLM engineering.

A Personal AI Career Agent

I used open-source tools to create a small, private AI that reads my complete LinkedIn export and helps me answer career-related questions in plain English.

It doesn’t make things up. It just searches my actual experience and gives clear, context-specific responses to questions like:

  • “What’s my leadership track record in digital health?”

  • “What experience do I have with international collaboration?”

  • “How does my research connect to real-world outcomes?”

It turns my LinkedIn data into something more useful: a storytelling tool. Not just a static profile, but a dynamic agent that helps me present the right parts of my story, depending on the question.

What Surprised Me Most

I didn’t expect it to teach me something about myself. But it did.

Patterns I hadn’t noticed. Connections I hadn’t made. And the confidence to say: yes, that is part of my story.

Why This Matters (Especially for Doctors)

If you practice in a second language like me, or your career doesn’t fit a single box (also like mine) this kind of tool can make a real difference.

It helps you:

  • Clarify your value across roles, systems, or specialties

  • Translate local experience into global relevance

  • Generate tailored responses for applications, panels, or networking

  • Feel more confident when describing your own path

It’s especially helpful for:

  • International doctors

  • Researchers moving toward industry

  • Clinicians navigating portfolio careers

And the tech behind it isn’t locked away. It’s built using tools anyone can access:

  • LinkedIn data export

  • ChromaDB (vector memory)

  • Sentence Transformers

  • Gradio interface

  • GPT-4 for natural language
