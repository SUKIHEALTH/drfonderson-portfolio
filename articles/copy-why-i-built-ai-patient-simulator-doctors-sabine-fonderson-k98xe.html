Why I built an AI patient simulator for doctors relocating to the Netherlands

Created on 2025-11-02 19:54

Published on 2025-11-03 09:45

If you are an international doctor or practising medicine in the Netherlands in a second language, you'll know this moment too well.

You've passed your Dutch language exams and you are more than ready to put all your knowledge and language skills to use.

Then you wait and wait until you are selected for the exams. According to sources who are going through the entire process, the waiting time is as long as 2 years.

In the meantime, there is little you can do. Even traineeships or shadow placements are scarce.

What do you do, you read books, guidelines but your confidence drops and you keep reading in silence.

After speaking to many doctors who have relocated to the netherlands, one missing piece of this complex puzzle was finding the right platform to practice medicine in the Netherlands.

That's why I built the AI Patient Simulator.

It is a fully interactive Dutch consultation tool where doctors relocating to the Netherlands can:

  • Talk to real-sounding dutch patients (AI-powered and aligned with NHG guidelines)

  • Practice Medisch Nederlands with direct feedback

  • Share transcripts of the conversation with a mentor or fellow student to keep engagements

  • And get that feeling of being in a consultation room, even before getting hired.

This isnt just another course.

This is a bridge between theory and practice. Stop waiting for a traineeship and start practicing AI-centered patient care as seen in consultation rooms.

If you know a doctor relocating to the Netherlands, tag them or share this post.

I am looking for 10 pilot testres to refine the next set of cases, from every day medical issues (kleine kwalen) to chronic care (ketenzorg).

It is not my intention to shorten the waiting period. All I hope this will achieve is to keep skills up and make it less foreign when you really enter a consultation room.

Try the simulator here: https://huggingface.co/spaces/sukihealth/academy
