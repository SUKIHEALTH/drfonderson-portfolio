Junior Doctors in UK leaving NHS

Dr. Sabine Fonderson

Physician · Health AI Innovator · Keynote Speaker

Created on 2015-09-29 07:47

Published on 2015-09-29 08:14

If you do not live in the United Kingdom, you may have missed the debates regarding the junior doctor contracts. I came across a forum (picture courtesy of Junior Doctor Contracts closed Facebook group) a week before many decided to demonstrate in Westminster last night.

The main cause of concern junior doctors have is the conditions of their work. In sum, they work unsociable, long hours which in turn pose safety concerns to themselves and more importantly to the patient they serve.

Regardless of the outcome, I want to say just how proud I am of junior doctors for taking a stand.

Not so long ago, I too was one of them, and I wish I had the strength and courage to stand up against a system that imposes unrealistic expectations more often than is necessary. 

I remember not being allowed to hand-over the care of a patient to other colleagues. The notion being ' you saw him, so you finish his care'. It did not matter that I had worked 12 hours with no more than 15 minutes break. I was exhausted and naturally just wanted to go home. Then the next day, the dreaded words 'Why did you not do xyz...that patient should have had abc done..'

I felt bad, my confidence was completely gone and that is how I would start my shift. But no one knew that because I stayed so late 'finishing' the care my patient, I missed my last train home. It would take me an extra 1,5 hours to finally get home, exhausted. By the time I would finally sleep, my alarm clock would go off.

The only words of comfort is that with time and experience, it does get better.

However, the biggest threat now in these forums is that many junior doctors are thinking of leaving the UK to pursue medical careers elsewhere.

My advice to all junior doctors considering a move else where is to do your research. There is a lot to consider

  • License exam take time and can be expensive
  • Entrance visa applications also take time and if you are not accepted, what other options do you have?
  • Where will you stay? do you have a social network in place that can help you transition?

There are other options for junior doctors to consider besides going abroad.
