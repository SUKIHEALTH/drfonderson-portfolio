Passion For business

Created on 2015-09-28 08:40

Published on 2015-09-28 08:48

Going to medical school and becoming a doctor was my dream but deep down  I also wanted to go into business.

By the time I was in my last year of medical school I had started an organisation called 'A Student's help in Africa'.

Nearly 10 years later I have sent over 280 students to Cameroon to do medical electives in rural hospitals. I did this next to my work as a busy doctor. It never bothered me because it was and still is something I am very passionate about.

I have started many other ventures, an online shop and an aesthetic clinic. Again I do this because I love the business side of me.

Doctors are diversifying their skills. I see that now more than ever. You can too!

Can you tell me what you are good at, what comes naturally to you?
