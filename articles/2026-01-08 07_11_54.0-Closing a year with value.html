My skills set turned into

  • AI patient simulators

  • Clinical note & report generators

  • R-code tools to help me as a researcher

  • Chrome extensions for my real GP problems

  • Creative scripts to produce AI generated art

  • Solutions to avoid frustrations when filling out questionnaires

  • Automation that resulted in over 500 tribunal cases, translated and summarised for my podcast - this alone saved me months of work

I did this because I am too curious and I believe that life is about finding balance and exploring each curiosity. With AI and the tools I have learned, I believe that I can truly be the best version of myself.
